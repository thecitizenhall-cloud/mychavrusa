The Chavrusa Platform

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026  ·  Contact: thechavrusaplatform@gmail.com

The short version: we collect only what's needed to match you with a chavrusa — your name, email, and study preferences. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't collect your phone number or address at intake. Email us to see or delete what we have.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the intake form, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Subject/topic you're looking to learn and any masechta or specific interest you list
  • Availability (days, time of day, session length)
  • Learning pace, style, and intensity preferences

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your home address or phone number
  • Payment information (the Service is currently free)
  • Any information from users under 18
  • Persistent location or device tracking

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to evaluate potential chavrusa matches during the current cohort and to contact you about your match status. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

The intake form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form backend. Your submission is transmitted to and stored by Formspree on our behalf; their handling of that data is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy. This site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of that request.

5. Data Retention

We retain intake submissions for as long as needed to run the current matching cohort and a reasonable period afterward for follow-up. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Sharing With Matched Users

If we identify a potential match for you, we share limited information from your intake submission (e.g. first name, subject interest, availability) with the other matched user so you can both decide whether to proceed. We will not share your email address or other contact information without your confirmation first.

7. Your Rights

You can request a copy of the information we have about you, or request that we delete it, at any time by emailing thechavrusaplatform@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we've collected information from a minor, we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to thechavrusaplatform@gmail.com.
