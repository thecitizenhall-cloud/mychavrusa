Common questions

What is The Chavrusa Platform?
It's a matching service that pairs working professionals with compatible chavrusas (Torah learning partners), matched by schedule, subject, pace, and learning style — not just whoever happens to be available.
How does the matching work?
You fill out a short adaptive intake covering your schedule, subject, session length, pace, style, and intensity. We look for compatibility across all of those and reach out when there's a genuine fit — with a clear explanation of why.
Is my name posted publicly anywhere?
No. Your name is never listed publicly or shown in any directory. When we find a potential match, each person first sees only the learning profile — schedule, subject, pace, and style — without any identity. Names are shared only if both people choose to connect.
Will my email be shared or sold?
Never sold, and never used for ads or marketing lists. We only share your contact details with a potential chavrusa if you confirm first — otherwise your email stays between you and us. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.
Who is it for?
Working professionals who want a reliable, compatible chavrusa but struggle to find one that fits their schedule, level, and learning style.
What can I learn?
Gemara (iyun or bekius), Daf Yomi, Halacha, Chumash with mefarshim, Mussar, Nach, and more. You can also name a specific masechta or topic during intake.
How long until I'm matched?
Our first cohort is still small, so it isn't always immediate. We won't pair you with someone just to fill a spot — we add new learners continuously and reach out the moment there's a real fit.
